# 💜 A 1990s Vintage Purple Letter for Ashlyn

A nostalgic, 90s-styled letter webpage in a royal purple & lavender aesthetic, created for your friend **Ashlyn**.

---

## 🌟 Refinements & Fixes

1. **Perfect Single-Page A4 PDF Export**:
   - Fixed text truncation: **100% of the letter is printed** (all 5 paragraphs, greeting, and signature).
   - Correct font size: Uses standard typewritten letter typography (`14.5px`, line-height `1.68`, `13px` paragraph spacing).
   - Balanced margins: Fits completely on **exactly 1 A4 portrait page** with no awkward top/bottom blank gaps or overflow pages.
   - Includes the authentic aged lavender parchment texture, postal stamp, header, fountain pen signature, and wax seal.

2. **Invisible Light Switch in the Dark**:
   - The light switch starts with `opacity: 0` and has no self-glow.
   - It **only illuminates and becomes visible/clickable** when your mouse flashlight beam shines directly over it!

3. **Invisible CC Trap (No Caught Point Box)**:
   - The visible caught point box has been removed.
   - Chasing and herding "CC" toward the top-right corner catches it naturally without any clutter on screen.
   - Transforms into **`Ching Chong.`** and loops dancing left and right!

4. **Very Small 2-Line Sound Control (Bottom-Right Corner)**:
   - **Line 1: `SFX`** — Volume slider & mute toggle for Rain & Thunder.
   - **Line 2: `MUSIC`** — Volume slider & mute toggle for the Christmas Song.

5. **Envelope & Verbatim Content**:
   - Envelope addressed to **Ashlyn** with strikeout on *~~handwritten~~*.
   - Postage: **`POSTAGE NO. 1671216`**.
   - Stamp: **`SHORTIE MAIL` / `NOV' EDITION`**.
   - Verbatim letter body text preserved 100% untouched.
